BIO-Manuel

Juan Manuel, or simply Manuel, is a young entrepreneur in his 20s who is just starting his professional career in the business world.

Originally from Africa and a Spanish speaker, he doesn't place importance on a person's origin, race, color, or ethnicity. He graduated with a degree in Computer Science from Tomsk State University (for those interested in his background).
Manuel has numerous promising business ideas and a roadmap that, with the necessary effort, dedication, and work, will yield significant benefits. Several of these ideas require a certain amount of initial investment and strategic partnerships to succeed. He plans to invest in each of these ideas in the future, as his primary goal is not to raise capital immediately. He has created Engage Digital Wealth, a Digital Marketing, Business and Technology company, which does not require a massive amount of capital to start, thus demonstrating his commitment to business. He doesn't plan on abandoning it for at least the next two decades.
Manuel understands that achieving his goals requires a strong work ethic, and he's willing to put in 70 to 100 hours per week to outpace the competition.
Before creating Engage Digital Wealth, Manuel worked as a marketing manager for several IT companies, including the Russian multinational Yandex, while also pursuing his studies. Thanks to these nearly three years of experience, he understands sales behavior, marketing strategies for business monetization, and customer acquisition.

After developing the blueprint, playbooks, business processes, and the Engage Digital Wealth website from scratch, among other things, Manuel has spent between 500 and 600 hours studying and working on monetization strategies for Engage Digital Wealth with his partners, striving to achieve the best possible results for them. This is not a game for him, even though he's in his 20s.

Manuel understands that his and his company's success depend on the success of his partners, clients, and allies. He is disciplined, communicative, and results-oriented, and he puts in 70 to 100 hours per week, turning his clients' problems into his own.
Manuel also hopes to make a significant contribution to society and help disadvantaged communities, such as the place where he came from, through various charitable works.
